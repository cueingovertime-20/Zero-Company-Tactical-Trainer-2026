STAR WARS Zero Company™ is a single-player, turn-based tactics game released on August 27, 2026. Developed by Bit Reactor in collaboration with Respawn Entertainment and Lucasfilm Games, it follows former Republic officer Hawks and an unconventional squad during the twilight of the Clone Wars.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **STAR WARS Zero Company™**. This trainer enhances the game's tactical campaign by modifying operator survivability, Action Points, Advantage generation, mission resources, and progression systems. It is designed for single-player operations, investigations, and squad management without changing the game's core turn-based structure.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with squad builds, explore the Clone Wars story, reduce the difficulty of tactical missions, test operator abilities, or replay campaign content with fewer resource and survival restrictions.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Keeps selected operators from losing health during tactical missions.
- **No Injuries** — Prevents operators from accumulating injuries when they are downed during missions.
- **Unlimited Mission Resources** — Maintains high values for campaign resources such as Intel, credits, and influence.
- **Fast Squad Progression** — Accelerates operator development, squad synergy improvements, and progression from completed missions.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Action Points** — Allows operators to move, attack, use abilities, and reposition without the normal three-AP limit.
- **Unlimited Advantage** — Provides continuous access to special actions that require Advantage points.
- **One-Hit Eliminations** — Greatly increases outgoing damage to help remove hostile units quickly.
- **Always Hit** — Removes the normal chance-to-hit uncertainty from ranged and melee attacks.
- **Freeze Enemy Turn** — Prevents opposing units from completing their normal turn while the option is active.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Consumables** — Keeps usable mission items and equipment supplies available during tactical operations.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Movement takes place on tactical maps where operators spend Action Points to reach positions, take cover, flank enemies, and secure objectives. Enable Unlimited Action Points before issuing movement commands if you want unrestricted repositioning. Avoid moving through scripted transitions while the option is active.

### Tools & Inventory

Operators use customizable loadouts, weapons, abilities, and cosmetic equipment. Campaign rewards and operations can provide items, credits, Intel, and influence. Unlimited Consumables is intended for mission equipment, while resource options are best used between operations when managing the squad at the Den.

### World & Environment

The campaign is organized around the Den, a decommissioned cargo freighter that serves as Zero Company's base. From the Holotable, Hawks selects missions and non-combat operations across the galaxy. Resource and progression options should be disabled before accepting story-critical assignments or ending a cycle.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based, with each operator normally receiving three Action Points for movement, attacks, abilities, overwatch, rallying allies, or calling for backup. Operators gain Advantage by damaging enemies and can spend it on powerful special actions. Activate health, accuracy, damage, or enemy-turn options before committing actions, and disable them before mission completion if you want normal rewards and progression.
